# Signal Run

Two seats, Human and Buddy, race to 7 points. Shuffle 18 cards: eight Charge (1 point), six Boost (2 points), and four Jam (1 point and subtract 1 from the other player, to a minimum of zero). Deal two cards to each seat. Human plays first. At the start of each turn, draw one card if the deck has any, then play one card from your hand. Played cards and their effects are public; hands and deck order are private. The first player to reach 7 wins immediately. If the deck and both hands are empty first, the highest score wins; a tie is a draw. If an empty-handed player has no deck to draw from, skip that seat. A player may never inspect the other hand or deck order through Buddy’s decision interface.

Examples: Charge at 2 scores 3; Boost at 5 wins at 7; Jam at 2 against 0 scores 3 and leaves the opponent at 0. With no cards remaining, a 5–5 score is a draw.
